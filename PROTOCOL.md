# FlashBlock minimum reproduction — frozen 2026-10-23

## Scope and question
Does block-external attention reuse reduce decoding cost on one RTX3090, and at what approximation/answer-quality cost? This is a resource-reduced, training-free mechanism/efficiency reproduction, not replication of the 8B distilled table. Negative results count. No training, quantization, video or SparseD extension.
